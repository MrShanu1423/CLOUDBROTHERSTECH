Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

CLOUDBROTHERSTECH aapki privacy ko seriously leta hai. Yeh policy batati hai ki hum kya data collect karte hain, kyun, aur aap isse kaise control kar sakte hain.

Jab aap CloudMitra se baat karte hain ya form fill karte hain, hum aapka naam, phone number, email, city, service interest aur koi additional message collect karte hain. Website visit karne par, hum basic analytics data (page views, browser type) bhi collect kar sakte hain.

Aapka data encrypted connections ke zariye transmit hota hai aur secured servers par store hota hai, jinhe hum khud firewall aur VPN se protect karte hain — wahi standard jo hum apne clients ke liye set up karte hain.

Aap kabhi bhi apna data access, correct, ya delete karne ki request kar sakte hain. Bas hamari team ko contact kijiye aur hum 7 working days ke andar respond karenge.
